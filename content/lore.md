## The Roadsters are here. Finally.

It is 2050. The first Tesla Roadsters have at last been delivered, and the Bay Area has become **Tesland**: a fictional future where Tesla and SpaceX run the show. Air tanks are as ordinary as parking tickets, and an enormous Elon Musk colossus looks out over the Golden Gate. The future has excellent branding.

You are a driver trying to make a living in the new Bay. Restaurants still have orders, customers still want dinner, and every shortcut has a price. Pick up a meal, beat the clock, get paid, and turn that cash into a better ride. On a good run, the food gets there before it gets cold. On a great run, you get there before the police.

## A city with more than one way through it

The streets still connect San Francisco, Oakland and the rest of the Bay. There are rooftops to reach, air currents to ride, cars to borrow and friends to meet along the way. Fly too close to the police and your shortcut might end with a costly citation. Take the ferry to Rocket Island if you need a break from respectable driving.

On the Marin side of the Golden Gate, the **Elon Colossus** keeps a watchful eye on the Bay. Nearby, the **First Delivery Monument** remembers the brave fictional courier who discovered that the shortest route to dinner is sometimes straight up. Look for their gold dots on the local map.

## The fine print of the future

Tesland is an independent work of fiction and satire, not a prediction or an official Tesla or SpaceX product. The ownership of the Bay, the monuments, flight, air refills and delivery economy are invented for the game. Real businesses and landmarks appear in a compressed, playful setting; no affiliation or endorsement is implied.
