# Embarcadero sky

`qwantani-sunset-1k.hdr`: [Qwantani Sunset (Pure Sky)](https://polyhaven.com/a/qwantani_sunset_puresky), photography by Greg Zaal and processing by Jarod Guest, provided by Poly Haven under [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). The 1024 × 512 HDR panorama is used for the scene's static environment reflections.

`qwantani-sunset-background.webp`: a display-referred 1024 × 512 derivative of the same panorama, locally tone-mapped and softly graded toward blue overhead while retaining the warm sun. `scripts/prepare-embarcadero-sky.py` records the conversion. Both the visible panorama and reflection environment use the same rotation as the directional sun and water shader. No outside network request is made at runtime.
