# Hosted preview and alpha playtest · October 18, 2026

This is a short feature demonstration, not a performance benchmark. On October 18 Pacific, we recorded the user-linked [radio preview](https://codex-tesland-radio-bay-drive.swyxio.workers.dev/?room=bay), which reported protocol 29 and served `index-DlaFxddQ.js`, and [the live alpha](https://tesland.swyx.io/), which displayed 0.0.34 alpha, reported protocol 47 and served `index-CMTxZOs6.js`. These hosted URLs can change; the observed bundle names identify what was loaded at capture time.

Both runs used fresh browser storage and separate newly named rooms, one driver each, on headless Playwright Chromium 156 with ANGLE Metal on an Apple M5 Pro at 1280 × 720. The checked-in [`blog-preview-comparison.json`](../../scripts/playtests/blog-preview-comparison.json) waits for the room, unlocks game audio, accelerates, holds jump/hover, releases the accelerator, brakes and switches camera. Capture used `pnpm playtest:record --url <hosted-url-with-fresh-room> --target-ref <observed-version> --scenario scripts/playtests/blog-preview-comparison.json --out <new-private-run-directory> --gpu metal --fps 30 --trajectory --audio`. Do not record other players or their chat without permission.

The rooms each reported the initial car at x=40, z=60. Comparing nearest room snapshots every half second from the beginning of recording through eight seconds gave a largest horizontal separation of about 9.9 m; at eight seconds it was about 0.8 m. The older run received 149 pose samples and the alpha 115 over their full runs. Snapshots are authoritative room positions, not displayed-frame positions, and the rooms, NPC traffic and clocks were independent. Earlier attempts diverged after a turn or after running farther; this shorter straight route was chosen after inspecting both recordings and trajectories. Requested recording FPS is not a measurement of game FPS.

The 8.47-second side-by-side combines the separate recordings at their beginnings. Its audio is the alpha's original synthesized game audio, aligned using the recorder's approximate 31 ms start offset and raised 5 dB; its measured peak is −11.3 dB. The separately linked preview and alpha MP4s each contain their own game audio. The separate podcast player, microphone and system audio were not captured. Encoding and audio synchronization are approximate; the [audio calibration](../VERIFICATION.md#audio-in-automated-playtest-videos--2026-10-18-pacific) characterizes the recording workflow, not this specific run. The source videos and screenshots were inspected for private content before publication.
