# Flight balance · 2026-10-18

These are deterministic shared-simulation measurements, not a browser or device playtest. The before source is main `dad912e19a1121420132959ab29fcc498c32837f`; the after source is this candidate. Both use the same [measurement script](../../../scripts/flight-balance-benchmark.ts), 50 ms steps, a single car and no traffic. [Before](before.json) and [after](after.json) contain the full Roadster and rocket-retrofitted Semi matrix, including stock and efficient nozzles, samples, altitude, distance and remaining air. Run `pnpm exec tsx scripts/flight-balance-benchmark.ts` on each checkout to reproduce it.

The measured hover begins stationary at 100 m with a full tank, no wind and hover held until empty. Values are seconds; air consumption is quantized to the 50 ms tick. An efficient nozzle reduces consumption 25%.

Before, both vehicles had identical flight. The new tank capacities are 50 / 100 / 200 / 400 / 800 units. A full Roadster jump costs 12 units, hover 18 units/s, and airborne forward thrust 4 units/s; nozzles multiply those costs by 0.75. The Semi's fictional flight load is capped at 1.2, increasing airborne consumption and gravity and decreasing jump and flight acceleration. These use in-game mass estimates and are not manufacturer claims.

With hover, forward thrust and fixed steering held together, these are horizontal **path lengths**, not straight-line range. The car follows a circular path and starts from rest at 100 m. Stock Roadster levels 1–5 change from 505 / 1,233 / 2,689 / 5,597 / 11,417 m to 42 / 153 / 505 / 1,233 / 2,689 m. Nozzle Roadster paths change from 749 / 1,717 / 3,657 / 7,537 / 15,293 m to 72 / 265 / 749 / 1,717 / 3,657 m. Stock Semi paths change from the same old Roadster values to 24 / 91 / 331 / 939 / 2,151 m; with nozzles they change to 42 / 158 / 535 / 1,343 / 2,959 m. Numbers are rounded to the nearest metre; the JSON retains hundredths.

A single Roadster jump still peaks at 5.91 m and lands at 2.20 s at every tank level. A Semi jump now peaks at 3.34 m and lands at 1.55 s, versus 5.91 m and 2.20 s before. Falling unpowered from 100 m takes 4.55 s for a Roadster and 4.15 s for a Semi, compared with 4.55 s for both before. A partial jump scales the entire change in vertical speed, so a nearly empty tank cannot cheaply cancel a fall.

At Level 1, the empty-tank Roadster placed in City Glide falls from 12 m and lands in 2.15 s while gaining 5% air; before it stayed in the lane 12.10 s and landed after 13.65 s with 84.53% air. An empty Roadster placed at 3 m in the thermal lands in 0.90 s; before it was still airborne at 15 s and had peaked at 102.89 m. Lanes now refill 2 air units/s and assist descent; the thermal refills 1 unit/s and adds 2.5 m/s² upward acceleration. Their assistance cannot hold an unpowered car against gravity.

In an additional [120 s, 20 Hz sweep](../../../scripts/flight-thermal-pulse-benchmark.ts) of repeated jump presses in the thermal, all 14 tested intervals of 0.1–5.0 s stayed airborne for the full 120 s in the [before result](thermal-pulses-before.json). The same inputs all landed with an empty tank in the [after result](thermal-pulses-after.json). The longest continuous airtime after was 13.15 s stock and 19.15 s with nozzles, both at 2.5 s between presses. This samples control patterns rather than proving an upper bound for every possible input. With one-second jumps, Level 4 stock reached the high Skyline in 24.65 s with 27.51% air; Level 2 stock reached Redwood City Caltrain in 6.65 s with 16.02%. Empty-tank gate and upstream-lane escapes remained possible in the tested scenarios. The L2 Semi cannot safely clear a stopped Robovan with one jump; both autonomous flight modes now reject that shortcut and leave control to the road navigator. If the obstruction remains, the existing navigation recovery can eventually report failure. The guard is an optimistic feasibility check; some other pre-existing autonomous jumps can still contact stopped traffic.

The synthetic 120 Hz presentation / 20 Hz authority motion traces use the unchanged prediction path. `scripts/motion-benchmark.ts --tank-level 4` keeps air available through the powered flight-turn interval in both runs. For steady simulated 150 ms round-trip transport, the Level 4 flight-turn's visual steering onset was 16.667 ms, one degree 58.333 ms and five degrees 191.667 ms in both traces; rendered/body-speed ratios p05–p95 were 0.983–1.058 in both. The ground fast-slalom and bunched flight trace metrics also matched before and after. The [before flight-turn report](before-flight-turn-steady150-level4-report.json) and [after flight-turn report](after-flight-turn-steady150-level4-report.json) summarize the results; the neighboring compressed traces retain full reports and events. These deterministic fixture results do not establish actual browser, GPU, network, physical-device or production performance.
