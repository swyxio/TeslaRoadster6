# Technology campuses

These are original low-poly, compressed game-space interpretations. Their footprint, orientation, building forms, surrounding roads and exact positions are fictional; they are not navigation data or affiliated venues. Labels use plain text and no downloaded logos, textures or brand assets. Shared rectangular building envelopes provide physical collisions and roof support. Apple Park uses a stepped ring of solid walls around a drivable courtyard, with a fictional south gate for ground access.

The real-world city identities are based on company sources:

- [Stripe](https://stripe.com/files/docs/terminal/m2_product_sheet.pdf), 354 Oyster Point Boulevard, South San Francisco.
- [Meta](https://about.fb.com/br/company.info/), Menlo Park; Facebook is part of Meta.
- [Google](https://about.google/company-info/locations/), Mountain View.
- [NVIDIA](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/contact/), Santa Clara.
- [Apple](https://www.apple.com/contact/), Cupertino; Apple's [description of Apple Park](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2017/02/apple-park-opens-to-employees-in-april/) describes its ring-shaped main building.

The Stripe, Meta, Google and NVIDIA silhouettes use fictional office blocks and original color accents. None reproduces an architectural survey. The Apple Park approximation uses a low-poly stepped ring rather than copying a model or photograph.
