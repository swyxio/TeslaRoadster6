# Bay geography and contributor contract

The map is a compressed, hand-authored arcade interpretation, not navigation data or a 1:1 recreation. Relative relationships follow the [Golden Gate Bridge District](https://www.goldengate.org/bridge/bridge-operations/traffic-management/) and [Metropolitan Transportation Commission](https://www.mtc.ca.gov/operations/programs-projects/bridges/san-francisco-oakland-bay-bridge): Golden Gate links San Francisco and Marin across the strait; the Bay Bridge links San Francisco and Oakland via Yerba Buena Island, with a western suspension section and an eastern span. Streets, coastlines, widths, elevation and landmark positions are artistic approximations.

`shared/map.ts` is the single authority for bounds, land polygons, road segments, bridge decks, parks and collision buildings. Positive X is east and positive Z south. `isSurface(x,z)` defines supported ground; every land polygon and both rectangular bridge decks support the vehicle pivot at Y=0. The visible water surface is Y=−4. Grounded vehicles stop at unsupported shores; an airborne entity landing in open water returns to a free spawn with air and condition preserved. `nearbyBuildings` is the shared spatial collision index. Physics, solo and Workers import the same data; clients never decide a landing or a hit.

`src/bay-world.ts` builds the landscape from those definitions, with instanced roads, buildings and windows. `src/main.ts` builds its minimap from the same polygons and roads. `shared/traffic.ts` owns SF/Oakland loops and a cross-Bay player AV route. Parked vehicles are collision-checked on creation; the first available spawn faces east toward the Bay Bridge. Tesla charger identities remain real, while compressed placement and air service are fictional. Nearby Oakland's charger is on the Oakland landmass.

- Land and bridge decks are currently flat. The Oakland approach ramp rises 4 m between x=370 and x=398 on z=180; its shared slope supports cars and launches them from the lip. The orange Oakland stunt loop is a 12 m radius, 12 m wide vertical ribbon centered at (450, 180); its analytic surface is shared by collision and rendering. Rocket fans adhere on contact, including the ceiling, independently of tank air. Other vehicles collide with its inside surface; this is a simplified arcade collision, not a general mesh solver. Water is visually lower; there is no swimming or boat physics, tidal simulation, elevated road grades, street elevation, SF hill driving, accurate Bay Bridge tunnel, or exact-sized landmark geometry.
- Building envelopes are physical; trees, piers, cranes, rails, cables, hill backdrop and charger posts are decorative. Do not create barriers on a playable road without matching shared collisions. Trees must stay clear of road segments.
- Any expansion of coastline, slopes, bridge ramps or obstacles must update shared geometry, minimap, simulation tests, and client/server together. Bump `PROTOCOL_VERSION` for incompatible world interpretation so stale clients reconnect. Preserve both crossings, charge sites, parked and NPC fleet, accessible spawn points, and mobile performance.
- Model artists may replace `src/bay-world.ts` visuals, but must keep those contracts and provide license/provenance plus desktop and touch playtests. Do not use a downloaded map or copyrighted texture without its license and attribution.
