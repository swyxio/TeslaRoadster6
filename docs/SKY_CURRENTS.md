# Sky currents

Fly into a blue moving lane to gain speed in its arrow's direction and replenish air. A lane gently holds altitude while the car remains inside. Golden rising rings mark thermals: jump into one to rise and refill air. Both work automatically for any airborne vehicle, including a stolen car without a rocket kit; a kit or Roadster helps reach them. Grounded cars and people are unaffected. Free air is capped at 100, and currents do not alter collision, police or delivery rules.

The low City Glide lane is 12 m above the Bay approach and can be reached with two jumps. Four higher lanes sit at 30, 38, 62 and 88 m. Thermals next to Coit Tower, the Transamerica Pyramid and Salesforce Tower start 2 m above the road and rise to 96 m; their centers are offset from the landmark collision boxes. Cars remain subject to the existing 100 m flight ceiling. The minimap shows blue dashed arrows and gold rings; the instrument panel identifies the current when occupied. These locations and heights are static for discoverability and deterministic multiplayer, not a live or random weather feed.

`shared/sky-currents.ts` is the source of truth for all volumes and `currentAt`. Server and solo simulation apply the same rules, and the renderer and HUD read those definitions; clients do not claim a current. Overlapping volumes resolve to a thermal first, then the first matching lane. Physics replenishes 14 air units per second, converted to a percentage of the installed tank's capacity; lanes accelerate along their direction up to a 60 m/s along-lane target without reducing existing faster motion and damp vertical motion; thermals apply upward acceleration with a 12 m/s pre-gravity target. All values are arcade tuning. Visual markers are replaceable and have no collision authority.

The National Weather Service describes [onshore winds through the Golden Gate](https://www.weather.gov/media/mtr/baybreezes/spotter_newsletter_fall09_web.pdf) and [thermals from surface heating](https://www.weather.gov/source/zhu/ZHU_Training_Page/turbulence_stuff/turbulence/turbulence.htm). These inspired the location choices; real thermals are weather-dependent, and tower locations do not imply guaranteed updrafts. These low-altitude arcade lanes are not real atmospheric jet streams. Air magically replenishing in wind is fictional. [Coit Tower's location](https://sfrecpark.org/Facilities/Facility/Details/Coit-Tower-290) and all landmark geometry are compressed game interpretations.

A future glider upgrade could trade powered lift for longer unpowered travel and reward finding thermals; it is not implemented or part of the current protocol.
