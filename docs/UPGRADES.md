# Rocket pickups and fictional workshops

Any of the 26 vehicles can become a rocket vehicle. Collect a purple `rocket` pickup in the city, on the island or at a parking playground for 90 seconds of flight and a full air tank, or buy a session-long kit at a purple workshop. A later pickup refreshes the temporary duration. The next-generation Roadster starts with its own fictional thrusters. A retrofit adds visible hardware to the car and uses the same jump, hover, boost and flip controls. Repair and air pickups stay green and amber; all pickups respawn after 20 simulation seconds.

The workshops are fictional SpaceX-branded game locations, not real facilities or an affiliation. The compressed map and sites are artistic placements. SF Mission and Richmond, Oakland Port, San Mateo, Palo Alto, Mountain View, San Jose, Fremont and East Bay each have a shop beside a street or highway. Purple diamonds mark them on the minimap. The location choices evoke industrial and roadside service areas; for example, the [City of Oakland describes the port's Gateway Industrial District](https://www.oaklandca.gov/Planning-Building/General-Plan-Neighborhood-Plans/Neighborhood-and-Citywide-Plans/Gateway-Industrial-District-Oakland-Army-Base). Do not use these positions as real-world navigation or facility data. Workshop gantries are decorative; the shared purple ground ring is the interaction area.

Stop the current vehicle in a ring and press **B**, use **Actions → Shop**, or tap the shop prompt. A shop spends the player's delivery cash immediately; the room validates the location, vehicle, motion, balance and upgrade before applying it. Costs and effects are game tuning:

The shop can also be opened from Actions anywhere to subscribe to the fictional SpaceX / Tesla OTA service. The opt-in costs $5 in delivery cash per game hour (120 real seconds); upgrades are charged separately. The first period is charged on enrollment if funds are available. If a renewal cannot be paid, access pauses without debt and automatically resumes with a fresh paid period when the player has $5. A paid period remains usable until its renewal; cancel anytime to stop future charges. Physical shops still work without a subscription. This is session-only game fiction, not a real subscription, payment, Tesla or SpaceX service. The remote-access pattern draws on [GTA Online's optional remote business access](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4WMldgJrkL3aVrIJNZN1E3/gta-benefits-for-accounts-with-multiple-grand-theft-auto-online-characters) and its [mechanic service pausing after an unpaid fee](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/6TVXdrdzZPNztlOSGWu1Y1/issues-accessing-the-mechanic-in-gta-online).

| Item | Cash | Effect |
|---|---:|---|
| Rocket kit | $60 | Session-long flight controls and a full air tank |
| Efficient nozzles | $45 | 25% less air for flight; requires stock or purchased rockets |
| Air tank levels 2–5 | $30 / $60 / $120 / $240 | Each doubles capacity and refills; requires stock or purchased rockets |
| Sport motor | $45 | 25% more acceleration and 10% higher road top speed |
| Impact armor | $35 | Half collision and hard-landing damage |
| Repair & refill | $12 | Restore condition and refill air; available when needed |

Every vehicle starts with a Level 1 tank at twice the original capacity; each upgrade doubles it again, for 2×, 4×, 8×, 16× and 32× original capacity. Air is shown as a percentage of the installed tank. A vehicle cannot buy a one-time upgrade twice. The Rocket Roadster cannot buy a redundant kit but can buy the other upgrades. Upgrades are attached to the individual vehicle, including a taken NPC car, and do not follow the driver. Selecting a Tesla creates a new personal car and removes the old personal car. Abandoned ambient cars can eventually be reclaimed by traffic management; all vehicles, upgrades and cash disappear when the room resets. Spendable cash is also the session leaderboard's balance, so purchases affect the ranking. There is no account, real payment or permanent vehicle upgrade.

The interaction draws on familiar in-world vehicle customization such as [GTA Online's vehicle shops](https://www.rockstargames.com/gta-online/special-interests/vehicles-enthusiast) and collectible driving boosts in [Rocket League](https://www.rocketleague.com/news/rocket-league-patch-notes-v2-53?lang=en). All mechanics, names, visuals and fictional flight are original game interpretations; no third-party game assets are included. Permanently equipped cars only consume a Rocket pad when their air is below full; it refills their tank. Full cars leave it available for others. Green repair and amber air diamonds restore their resource and award $5 session cash; that cash does not count as delivery earnings for unlocks.
