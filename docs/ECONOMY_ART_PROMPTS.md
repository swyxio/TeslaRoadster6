# Economy article art

The article's illustrations were generated with the built-in image generation tool. Final workspace files and prompts are recorded here so the series can be extended consistently. The annotated `public/blog/economy/bay-landmarks.svg` is drawn separately from `shared/map.ts` by `pnpm exec tsx scripts/economy-map.ts`.

## `public/blog/economy/cybercab-concept.webp`

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide editorial image for a game-development article about Tesland. Primary request: a custom-branded autonomous Cybercab cruising through a sunny, compressed San Francisco street, shown in a close three-quarter view. The car has a distinctive yellow-gold wedge silhouette, clean black glass, wheels, and a large, clearly visible geometric mint-and-coral custom decal across its doors, with a small floating owner-name plaque above the car. The plaque contains exactly "MAYA'S CAB". Scene/backdrop: simple Bay Area waterfront and pale blocky buildings in the distance; a few small autonomous cars on the road. Style/medium: polished stylized 3D game concept art, toy-like low-poly world, crisp silhouettes; based on an existing arcade game whose visual direction is warm coastal daylight, desaturated sea/teal, cream buildings, amber actions, mint markers. Believable vehicle proportions. Composition: wide landscape, strong car silhouette with the entire car in frame, details of the decal readable; article illustration with no interface overlay. Lighting/mood: warm coastal afternoon, optimistic and playful. Constraints: image is concept art rather than a screenshot; avoid fake menus, other text, watermark, photographic realism, logos and brand trademarks.
