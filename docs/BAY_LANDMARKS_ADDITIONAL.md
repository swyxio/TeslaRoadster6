# 100 additional Bay Area landmarks

Researched 2026-10-18. Each named place below inspired original procedural art; linked sources identify the real place. Locations, size, land and nearby roads are deliberately compressed for play, not geographic or architectural surveys. Richmond and Alameda are represented on the existing East Bay mainland; Muir Woods and other Marin places are remixed into the small Marin headlands, and coastal/outlying sites such as Pigeon Point and Lick Observatory are relocated within the supported world. Some venues may be closed or changed; their inclusion identifies a place, not current access.
