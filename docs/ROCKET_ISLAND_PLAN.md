# Rocket Island and parking playgrounds · implementation status

This protocol v25 checkout combines the Rocket Island playground and cockpit view with the China Basin Rocket Dome, Rocket pickups and fictional shops. These are fictional, compressed game locations and do not represent real vehicle capabilities. The public deployment may still run an earlier revision. See [VERIFICATION.md](VERIFICATION.md) for observed checks and [CONTRACTS.md](CONTRACTS.md) for protocol and implementation details.

## In the current checkout

- **Island and ferry:** The island has a water gap, an SF dock and an autonomous ferry controlled by the simulation. Its 38 × 14 m deck supports up to six cars, plus people. Board while docked; cars must be fully on the deck and stopped for departure. Riders remain attached and cannot drive or exit while crossing. After arrival, riders can leave; remaining passengers move to checked shore positions after a timeout, or the ferry waits if the apron is blocked. HUD, minimap and dock signs show ferry state; the deck has painted walking lanes, pedestrian standing spots and gates that open visually at the dock, and a nearby synthesized horn sounds on departure and arrival. The markings and gates are visual cues, not collision geometry. The simulation uses a 30-second boarding window and ordinary simulation time.
- **Safety and delivery:** The island polygon and ferry support clear pursuits and prevent police detection. The current order timer pauses there, without affecting cockpit order eligibility; active-play time and global cockpit active time retain their usual rules. Recovery and vehicle selection use checked local island positions where available.
- **Rocket pads:** Purple pickups throughout the Bay, on the island and at the registered parking playgrounds refill a driven vehicle's tank and grant 90 simulation seconds of fictional Rocket capability. Pads return after 20 seconds; another pickup refreshes rather than stacks the duration. Contention is settled by vehicle ID. The stock Rocket Roadster and purchased shop kits keep their capability for the session. Effects and expiry belong to the vehicle, not its driver.
- **Bank and cliff:** The speed oval has physical banking and shoulders that taper to ground; its start/grid, short-course overlap and dock junction remain flat with gradual transitions. The short circuit remains flat. A separate banked lane and a 24 m-wide inverted cliff also use shared geometry for rendering and physical support. The cliff sweeps about 225° around a 20 m radius. Rocket fans can adhere beyond vertical; ordinary cars fall or bounce. Steering and traction use the arcade handling model, so banking does not promise realistic high-speed grip.
- **Jump ramps:** Two orange eastbound ramps in the island infield launch ordinary and Rocket cars. Their shared 4 m lips preserve upward slope momentum and their swept surface contacts support landings; their approaches and fast landing corridors clear the other activities.
- **Racing:** The short circuit and speed oval have optional, simulation-owned lobbies for up to eight drivers, a 15-second lobby, three-second countdown and directional 3D checkpoints. Joining assigns a checked start position; a host can start early. Cars remain physical and can bump. Results and timers are session-only.
- **Parking:** The shared registry contains two playgrounds, one in SF and one on the island, and four Supercharger lots. All six support Park It and Cut the queue; the Mini-games picker can take a car to a checked starting position. Park It requires a complete car footprint, heading and speed to satisfy a stall for two seconds. Queue play records arrival order and awards eligible stalls. These games are opt-in and do not create persistent rewards.
- **Rocket Dome:** The China Basin soccer arena is connected to SF streets by a drivable entrance and has direct, reversible travel from the Bay or Rocket Island. One shared ball and blue/orange goals serve all players without assigned teams; either side reaching five wins, followed by an automatic reset. Rocket-capable cars can adhere to its curved walls and ceiling. Delivery order time pauses and police pursuit clears in the dome.

The world clock accelerates the sky and traffic demand, not ferry travel, upgrades, physics or activity timers. Solo time advances while its simulation runs; an empty or restarted room does not accumulate simulation time.

## Remaining work and evidence

- Pedestrian lanes and standing spots are marked but not reserved. The ferry's six-car rule is a gameplay constraint, not a measured throughput guarantee; blocked/full-deck and all-vehicle boarding behavior need further end-to-end playtesting.
- The six registered lots share the same controller. A 2026-10-18 source audit found no other authored land-based parking lots or Supercharger lots; ambient parked cars and the ferry's marked vehicle spaces do not define additional playgrounds. Reachability and play at every lot remain unverified, and queue play has no scripted NPC queue. Add future playable lots to the same shared registry.
- The bank, cliff, race courses, ferry crossing and parking games need full desktop and touch playthroughs, including joining, leaving, congestion and recovery. Combined island, dome and upgrade interactions, end-to-end two-client ferry crossings and actual physical-device touch remain unverified unless a later dated entry records them.
- Verify late-joining and disconnecting passengers, blocked destinations, all 26 vehicle footprints and temporary pickup expiry during a crossing or on the overhang with real play sessions. Tune heavy vehicles as arcade behavior, never as real-world performance.
- Measure peak traffic, snapshots and rendering with occupied ferry and active games before making capacity or performance claims. Local tests and room smoke checks do not establish production, sustained or mobile capacity.
