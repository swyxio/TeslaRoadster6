# Bay highway performance observations · 2026-10-18 Pacific

Two sequential, no-video pairs used clean base `fa1713cda3335e0caa1b798e3025e22e8e51ba76` (0.0.39-alpha, protocol 50) and clean candidate `a1358fe83147eb2657bd815d4d437b86eacb9eb1` (0.0.40-alpha, protocol 51). Both used local Wrangler and 1280 × 720 headless Chromium on an Apple M5 Pro with 18 logical CPUs and ANGLE Metal. Each run had one fresh guest, no video and the same fixture on both sides. The pair wrappers document the original commands, paths and disk guard. The retained reports contain fixture hashes, browser/host conditions and measurements; `SHA256SUMS` covers all retained evidence except this README.

The standard delivery-and-flight pair aligned across all five distance-normalized legs: 1.213 m overall p95 and 1.849 m maximum separation. Browser animation callback interval p95 was 16.8 / 16.8 ms; room simulated / wall time was 0.953 / 0.955. One-minute host load before → after was 9.894 → 8.761 / 8.368 → 7.166. Its comparison remains HOLD for general performance claims: one pair, different host load and a partly stationary staging wait are not a regression verdict. See `standard/comparison.md`.

The separate seeded highway pair started at `(-798, 3300)`, yaw 0.053, beyond the fresh guest's normal region gate. The ordinary live room ran KeyV lane following for 64 seconds. The paths diverged by 34.45 m p95 and 36.27 m maximum at distance-normalized samples, exceeding the predeclared 25 m p95 / 50 m maximum limits. The comparison is **HOLD**, so the raw callback p95 of 16.7 / 16.7 ms and room simulated / wall ratios 0.957 / 1.018 must not be treated as a matched highway performance result. Route lengths were 1,460 / 1,424 m. One-minute host load before → after was 5.92 → 8.30 / 8.18 → 7.71. Minimum recorded free disk during the pair was about 2.80 GB; neither disk guard fired. See `highway/comparison.json` and the retained trajectories.

A post-hoc inspection found that the fixtures gave the two cars different random IDs, and cruise speed depends on that ID. Resampling all pose samples at 401 identical absolute distances within the shorter path gave 0.030 m p95 / 0.129 m maximum separation; this is a diagnostic and does not change the predeclared HOLD. The candidate's first KeyV gesture took 4.136 seconds and included an initial 4.117-second animation interval, versus 0.141 and 0.117 seconds in the baseline; the cause is unknown. The standard pair's first gestures were both about 0.15 seconds. The candidate room ratio is misleading: the first selected snapshot arrived 4.145 seconds after recording began, followed by about 3.8 simulated seconds of stationary snapshots in a 5 ms burst. The metric omits the initial gap. KeyV toggles ordinary lane following, not delivery route planning; the captured evidence cannot locate the delay within input dispatch, page handlers, rendering, audio initialization or host scheduling. The highway recording did not retain nearby vehicle positions, so it cannot prove traffic exposure or rule out traffic effects. This route also did not exercise a merge, police chase or highway incident.

A later six-leg startup diagnostic stopped after three legs. The two complete base / candidate observations had first-gesture durations of 288 / 222 ms and maximum animation intervals of 284 / 227 ms; the third candidate observation was censored because its clocks could not be aligned within the preregistered bound. The original four-second pause did not recur in the two complete observations, but its cause remains unknown and the highway comparison remains HOLD. `startup-attempts/` retains the stopped attempts, partial results and executed harness; the earlier attempts stopped before driving and do not measure gameplay.
