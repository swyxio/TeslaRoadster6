# Recorded cross-version route · 2026-10-18

Separate recordings of base 0.0.31-alpha `26fc934` and candidate 0.0.32-alpha `4d7cee4` completed the controlled two-delivery route, two jumps, landings and forward-powered flight. All five legs aligned, with sampled path separation p95 1.379 m and maximum 2.04 m. Both manifests report zero page and console errors.

Recording changes the workload; these videos do not supply a performance delta or a faithful measure of player experience. Both recorded runs had a 116.6 ms p95 browser callback interval on the heavily loaded virtual Mac; the output's requested 30 fps does not establish 30 distinct rendered frames. The route spends most of its time driving and does not deliberately hold boost, so it is also a weak visual comparison for boost and landing. Use it as route evidence. The [original video workflow summary](video-summary-original.md.gz), reports, manifests, trajectories and input events are retained separately from the no-video pair.
