# Northern Bay landmark references

Researched 2026-10-18. These are 50 **additional** place identities, plus two natural features, on top of the game's existing Bay bridges, Coit Tower, Ferry Building, Transamerica Pyramid, Salesforce Tower, Tribune Tower and Berkeley Campanile. Their small game-space positions, dimensions and low-poly geometry are invented to fit the compressed map; they are neither navigation data nor architectural surveys. No third-party models, logos, photographs or textures are included. Named parks, streets, buildings and structures are inspirations and do not imply affiliation.

The common silhouettes are deliberately simplified: columns, domes, pitched roofs, theatres, gates, tower frames, ships and terraces have collision envelopes in the shared map. Tiny signs are original canvas text, grouped into one texture. Decorative features inside a footprint do not create separate physics. Twin Peaks and Mount Tamalpais instead use the shared sloped terrain and decorative signs, not collision boxes. The game does not reproduce their actual elevation or surrounding terrain.

## Natural features (2)

[Twin Peaks](https://www.sfrecpark.org/facilities/facility/details/Twin-Peaks-384) and [Mount Tamalpais](https://parks.ca.gov/?page_id=28500) are represented by small, invented, drivable shared hills. They do not reproduce real elevations, roads or coastlines.
