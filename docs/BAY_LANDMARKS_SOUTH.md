# Peninsula, South Bay and Fremont landmark references

Research checked 2026-10-18. These 50 recognizable names are represented by original, compact low-poly interpretations, not surveyed models. They are deliberately redistributed next to the game's compressed roads. A nearby nameplate helps players identify each place; no site operates in-game and no affiliation is implied. Each model is a visual shorthand: gardens use a small pavilion or marker; museums and stations are closed structures rather than functional interiors. The SFO museum is represented as a separate roadside building for visibility even though the real museum is in the airport.

## Peninsula (13)

- [South San Francisco City Hall](https://www.ssf.net/files/assets/public/v/1/our-city/documents/city-hall.pdf); [SFO Aviation Museum](https://www.flysfo.com/passengers/services-amenities); [Burlingame Train Station](https://www.burlingame.org/1352/Historic-Preservation); [CuriOdyssey](https://www.smcgov.org/parks/coyote-point-recreation-area-activities).
- [San Mateo Japanese Garden and San Mateo City Hall](https://www.cityofsanmateo.org/index.aspx?NID=3319); [San Mateo County History Museum and Redwood City Fox Theatre](https://www.redwoodcity.org/about-the-city/visiting/accommodations-restaurants).
- [Pulgas Water Temple](https://www.sfpuc.gov/about-us/news/first-drop-lasting-legacy-pulgas-water-temple-honored-water-landmark); [Filoli Historic House](https://filoli.org/visit/); [Hiller Aviation Museum](https://www.hiller.org/visit/); [Allied Arts Guild](https://alliedartsguild.org/architecture/); [Palo Alto HP Garage](https://ohp.parks.ca.gov/ListedResources/Detail/976).

## Stanford (8)

[Stanford Visitor Information](https://visit.stanford.edu/explore-campus) identifies Memorial Church, Main Quad, the Oval and Palm Drive, the Dish, the Mausoleum and the Arizona Cactus Garden. [Stanford's visitor guide](https://www.stanford.edu/life/visit) identifies Cantor Arts Center, and [Stanford Arts](https://arts.stanford.edu/for-visitors/venues-and-facilities/) identifies Bing Concert Hall. These are distinct sites in addition to the existing Hoover Tower landmark.

## Mountain View (6)

[The City of Mountain View](https://www.mountainview.gov/whats-happening/discover-mv) identifies Computer History Museum, Shoreline Amphitheatre, Rengstorff House and Mountain View Center for the Performing Arts; the city also documents its [Historic Adobe Building](https://www.mountainview.gov/our-city/departments/community-services/recreation/facilities-rentals/historic-adobe-building). [NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/history/hangar-one-restoration-project/) describes Moffett Field Hangar One.

## San José (15)

The city's [downtown plan](https://www.sanjoseca.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/38781/638058306327430000) identifies The Tech Interactive, Cathedral Basilica of St. Joseph, San José Museum of Art, SAP Center, Peralta Adobe, San José State Tower Hall, Children's Discovery Museum, California Theatre and Diridon Station. The city's [downtown map](https://www.sanjoseca.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/9865/636657352348370000) additionally identifies Winchester Mystery House, Rosicrucian Egyptian Museum, San Pedro Square Market and the Municipal Rose Garden. The city also documents [Happy Hollow Park and Zoo and the Japanese Friendship Garden](https://www.sanjoseca.gov/Home/Components/FacilityDirectory/FacilityDirectory/2835/2028).

## Fremont (8)

The City of Fremont identifies [Patterson House at Ardenwood, Shinn House, Vallejo Adobe and California Nursery Historical Park](https://www.fremont.gov/government/departments/parks-recreation/parks/historic-parks-facilities); [Niles Depot, Niles Essanay Silent Film Museum and Aqua Adventure](https://www.fremont.gov/about/attractions). The [East Bay Regional Park District](https://www.ebparks.org/parks/mission-peak) documents Mission Peak Regional Preserve. The in-game summit marker is an artistic abbreviation and is placed on shared terrain, not a surveyed reproduction.
